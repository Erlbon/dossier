Dossier for the Mac (Apple Silicon: M1 and later). A first test build: not signed by Apple, and it does not update itself.

The Mac stops an app that Apple has not signed the first time it is opened. To get past that, once:
1. Unzip, drag Dossier into Applications and open it. The Mac says it cannot check it: click Done.
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy and Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway beside Dossier.
3. Click Open (the Mac may ask for your password). From then on Dossier opens like any other app.

Good to know
- The F-keys (F5 copy, F6 move, F7 new folder, F8 delete) need Fn on most Mac keyboards, unless "Use F1, F2, etc. keys
  as standard function keys" is on in System Settings, Keyboard. Where Windows says Ctrl, press Cmd.
- The first time Dossier opens Terminal or iTerm for you, the Mac asks whether Dossier may control it.
- Help > Check for Updates says whether there is a newer build and links to it; it does not install it.
- Please write down anything that looks wrong or works differently from what a Mac user expects.
