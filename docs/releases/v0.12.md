Dossier 0.12: the picture viewer grows up. Delete to the Recycle Bin, turns kept in the photo, a card with the photo's facts, instant stepping, gliding zoom, moving GIFs, Open With, a slider for long folders, and the glass bar's menus in its own look.

**Two downloads.** `dossier-setup.exe` installs Dossier for you alone (no administrator rights, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Dossier`) and starts in under a second. `dossier.exe` is the one-file program: nothing to install, but it unpacks itself at every start and takes about three seconds. Windows 10 or 11. Settings stay in your user profile (`dossier.ini`); 0.11 settings carry over.

## The picture viewer

All of this is in View as Glass, `dossier view` and pictures in F3, unless it says otherwise.

- **Delete** sends the picture to the Recycle Bin with no question, says so at the top ("IMG_4412.jpg is in the Recycle Bin") and shows the next one. On a drive with no Recycle Bin (a network drive, a USB stick) it asks first and says it is for good. Put Back in the Recycle Bin place on the Home page brings a picture back; Ctrl+Z does not.
- **A turn of a JPEG is kept.** L and R write the turn into the photo's EXIF at once, without writing the picture again, and the file keeps its date. Other kinds of picture turn for looking only, and the viewer says so the first time.
- **The photo's facts** (I, or the new ⓘ button in View as Glass): a small card at the top right with the name, size, date taken, camera and lens, and exposure. It stays while you step through the folder until you press I again, and is remembered. F3 keeps its full I panel.
- **Stepping is instant:** the next and the previous picture are read while you look at this one.
- **Zoom, fit and 1:1 glide** there in about a sixth of a second, toward the point under the mouse.
- **Animated GIF and WebP play**, round and round. Space pauses them (Space still steps past a still picture).
- **Open With** in the gear menu lists the same programs as the panel's Open With, and the viewer stays open. **E** opens the picture at once in a program you choose once in Settings > Opening Files > Edit Pictures With.
- **A slider for long folders**, above the bar: drag it to see "331 / 870 · name", and only the picture you let go on is read. A click on it jumps there.
- **A click on the picture shows the bar and keeps it**; the next click hides it. The bar takes no clicks while it is still fading in, so a click near the bottom no longer turns a picture on a button you could not see.
- No scroll bars on a zoomed picture in View as Glass; dragging, the arrows and the wheel move it.

## Also new since 0.11

- The glass bar's menus (the viewer's gear, the notepad's File, Edit, View and gear) are in the bar's own rounded look and open just above the bar.
- The notepad's tabs look like the panels' tabs: rounded, the current one gold, with the same small cross. A middle-click closes a tab.

## Things to know

- **Coming from 0.11:** Help > Check for Updates does it.
- The exe and the installer are not code-signed, so SmartScreen may ask the first time.
- **Not yet tried by hand:** the new viewer parts were tested with stand-ins, not on a real desktop: Delete into the real Recycle Bin and on a real network drive, turns on photos from real cameras, Open With and E starting real programs.
- There is no slideshow and no star rating in the viewer, on purpose.

Every change, build by build, is in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Erlbon/dossier/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
